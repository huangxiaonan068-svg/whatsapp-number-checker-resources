# WhatsApp Number Checker Resources

A practical collection of resources for cleaning, formatting, and validating WhatsApp contact lists before starting outreach or marketing campaigns.

## Why Clean Your WhatsApp Contact List?

Contact lists exported from CRM systems, spreadsheets, lead databases, or event registrations often contain:

* Invalid phone numbers
* Incorrect country codes
* Duplicate contacts
* Numbers that are no longer active
* Numbers that are not registered on WhatsApp

Cleaning the list before sending messages can reduce wasted outreach and make contact management easier.

## Basic WhatsApp Number Format

WhatsApp numbers should generally use the international phone number format:

```text
Country Code + Phone Number
```

Examples:

```text
United States: +12025550123
United Kingdom: +442071234567
Brazil: +5511987654321
India: +919876543210
```

Avoid spaces, brackets, and unnecessary symbols when preparing a CSV contact list.

## Recommended Workflow

1. Export your contact list as CSV or Excel.
2. Remove duplicate records.
3. Normalize phone numbers into international format.
4. Validate the numbers with a WhatsApp number checker.
5. Remove invalid or unavailable numbers.
6. Import the cleaned list into your outreach workflow.
